About 01

I am a Research Scientist at Anuttacon, working on real-time voice agents — voice interaction models and large audio language models (LALMs). I have hands-on ownership across pretraining, post-training (SFT & RL), agent interaction design, and evaluation.

I hold an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from CMU and a B.S. in Statistics & Computer Science from UIUC. At CMU I worked at WAVLab under Prof. Shinji Watanabe, on efficient inference for speech language models and unified audio-visual understanding.

My research interests are general speech and audio language models, audio-visual fusion, and multimodal language models — increasingly, how the harness around a model shapes what the model needs to be.

During my undergraduate studies I was fortunate to work with Prof. Tarek Abdelzaher, Prof. Kris Hauser, and Prof. Yuxiong Wang, which greatly shaped my research journey.

I'm happy to collaborate on interesting projects. If you'd like to chat, just drop me an email.

Experience 03
  • Jan 2025 — Present Research Scientist, Anuttacon — Mountain View, CA Voice agent post-training. I own the post-training stage of the company's voice agent model — hundreds of billions of parameters trained on thousands of GPUs — driving full-cycle iteration from training recipe design through evaluation. Built the post-training data pipeline and delivered roughly 5–20% gains on audio benchmarks (MMAU, BigBench Audio, Audio MultiChallenge) while also strengthening text reasoning and math.

    Agent interaction design. Designed an agent harness system defining how the real-time speech interaction model coordinates with the backend main agent and the user — task delegation, asynchronous result integration, proactive interjection. The harness in turn shaped the training-data patterns, evaluation design, and product architecture.

    Large Audio Language Model. Led research and development of the company's general audio understanding model across speech, audio and music, reaching performance comparable to the state of the art at the time, and integrated it into the data synthesis and filtering pipelines behind the voice agent.

    Evaluation framework. Built evaluation infrastructure for fast, comprehensive assessment of very large models, aggregating public and internal benchmarks across understanding, generation and dialogue; proposed new evaluation paradigms for multi-turn spoken dialogue.

    Shipped product. Developed the ASR system for Whispers from the Star, supporting robust on-device speech recognition in production.
